How it works

A light, daily cycle that closes the follow-up gap.

Denizen integrates with your EHR through a daily report, runs structured patient outreach throughout the day, and reports back to your team each evening. Your team's workflow doesn't change.

One-time setup

About 30 minutes with your office manager.

We configure a daily report from your EHR — same data your team already pulls, just scheduled to deliver to us each morning. No installs on your end. No integration project. No vendor onboarding.

Each day

The daily cycle.

1 — Morning

EHR delivers the patient report

Securely via SFTP. Includes today's active prenatal and postpartum patients and the touchpoints due.

2 — Triage

We plan the day's outreach

Each patient is routed to the right touchpoint based on stage, history, and risk profile. Out-of-scope cases route back to your team before any outreach happens.

3 — Outreach

An RN reaches out

By phone or text, 1-on-1. Trained to OB-GYN follow-up protocols. Tier 2 touchpoints get RN review before any message is sent.

4 — Escalation

Anything urgent routes to you

Within stated service levels — 1 business hour for response, 30 minutes for clinical escalation, immediate paging for critical signals.

5 — Evening

End-of-day digest

Patient status, flagged concerns, and what needs your attention — delivered to your team. Two-way close: every outreach event lands back in your view of the patient.

What's covered

Prenatal, postpartum, and follow-up touchpoints.

Our scope is the structured follow-up calendar of obstetric care — the touchpoints that benefit from a human voice but rarely get one consistently.

Prenatal check-ins

Trimester-aligned outreach. Symptom tracking, education, and screening question follow-ups.

Lab + result follow-ups

Communicating results, answering questions, and confirming next steps in plain language.

Vaccination + appointment reminders

Tdap, flu, RSV, and recurring visit reminders timed to clinical milestones.

Postpartum follow-up

Day 3, 10, 21, and 6-week structured check-ins. Mental health screening included.

Pre-hospital prep

Hospital pre-registration, pediatrician selection, GBS results, and birth-plan touchpoints.

Sensitive-case routing

Loss, fetal anomaly, and other sensitive ICD-10 categories are filtered out of automated outreach and surfaced to your team only.

What's not covered

We don't replace your clinical team. We extend it.

Denizen is a follow-up service, not a tele-health provider. Clinical decisions, prescriptions, scheduling, billing, and ongoing in-person care stay where they belong — with your practice.
