About us

Built by home-care operators.

Aponot was founded by a team that has run high-performing home-care agencies from the inside. We started it to bring non-skilled home care into value-based care — and finally pay for the outcomes it quietly produces every day.

Our story

Aponot was started by a team that met at MIT and spent years as operators in the home-care industry. We’ve run high-performing home-care agencies, so we know how to deliver industry-leading care, support caregivers through a demanding role, and navigate the complex systems — Medicaid, long-term-care insurance, managed care — that surround it.

Running those agencies, we saw the same thing over and over: the daily, in-home work our caregivers did quietly prevented expensive acute events — the fall caught early, the medication error spotted, the decline flagged before it became an ambulance ride. But nobody paid for that value. Non-skilled home care sat outside value-based care entirely, billed by the hour, disconnected from the outcomes it produced.

We built Aponot to close that gap: to aggregate home-care agencies into a single network, overlay what a decade of value-based care has already proven, and connect the home to the payers who carry the cost of avoidable care — so the value our caregivers create every day finally shows up where it should.

We build from inside the work, not above it. Aponot is built on CareUp, our own licensed Florida home-care agency — so every protocol and product decision starts from how care actually gets delivered.

What we believe

A few things we build everything around.

01

Home care is an underused clinical lever

A caregiver in the home is the highest-frequency touchpoint a high-risk senior has. The moments that prevent an ED visit or an admission happen there — long undervalued because no one paid for them.

02

Pay for outcomes, not hours

Care should be rewarded for what it produces, not just the time it bills. That shift is what finally makes better care and a better business line up in the same direction.

03

Build on what’s already proven

A decade of value-based care showed which activities actually move outcomes. We don’t reinvent that playbook — we bring it into the one setting that can run it every day.

04

Operators, not spectators

We’ve run the agencies and delivered the care ourselves. We design Aponot for how care actually happens on the ground — not how it looks on a slide.
