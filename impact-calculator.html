These are deliberately conservative, directional estimates and vary by industry and workforce. We assume roughly 1 in 4 employees is an active family caregiver, and that each loses on the order of 10 productive workdays a year to caregiving — missed days and lost focus combined (Gallup puts documented absence alone near 7). That lost time is valued at roughly 40% of revenue per employee — not the full figure — to reflect the margin labor actually drives, not top-line revenue. Turnover exposure assumes only a small share of caregivers leave each year for caregiving reasons. Aponot's cost assumes ~$5 per employee per month. Sources: Gallup, AARP, SHRM, and Harvard Business School's The Caring Company.
