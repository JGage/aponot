The problem inside your workforce

47% of adults in their 40s and 50s have a parent age 65 or older and are actively caring for them.

For most, that care is invisible to their employer — until it isn't. Missed meetings. Distracted workdays. A resignation letter.

The cost of caregiver-related absenteeism and turnover in US workplaces is measured in the tens of billions annually.

Elder care benefits are becoming a competitive advantage

Demand is climbing fast — but supply hasn't kept up. Only about 1 in 8 US employers offers any eldercare benefit today, and most of those are just referral lines, not actual care. That gap is the opportunity: the employers who deliver real care now are the ones who stand out to the experienced, sandwich-generation talent everyone is fighting to keep.

But most current offerings fall short of what employees actually need.

Navigation-only apps
Guidance, but no delivery. A coordinator helps the employee think through options — then leaves them to find, vet, and arrange the actual care on their own.
Companion visits
Social visits to the parent. Friendly, valuable for isolation — but not care. No help with bathing, mobility, medication, or the moments that actually cause a family crisis.
Referral lists
A vetted list of local agencies. The employee still calls around, screens providers, negotiates rates, and hopes someone picks up.
Each solves a slice. Aponot is the only one that pairs concierge care navigation — a real advisor guiding the family from the very first question — with real, licensed home care delivered in the home when it's needed.
How Aponot works

Aponot works on two levels. A concierge care advisor helps employees plan and navigate elder care from the very first question — often years before hands-on help is needed, and across everything from assisted living to aging-in-place planning. And when the day comes, licensed home care is delivered in the home. The steps below show that delivery path.

1

Request

Employees log in and describe what their loved one needs — often in minutes. No phone tag, no hunting for an agency.

2

Match

Aponot matches them with a vetted local agency and arranges a credentialed, background-checked caregiver.

3

Care

Care is delivered in the home. Aponot handles scheduling, payment, and follow-up from there.

Employers pay a predictable subscription. Employees can opt into additional coverage tiers via voluntary payroll deduction.
