The problem

Approximately 95% of OB-GYN practices don't execute structured patient follow-ups.

The result is poor outcomes for both the patient — health, mental health, experience of care — and the practice — operational drag, missed visits, and lost revenue. The gap is well-documented; the bandwidth to close it is not.

What we do

Denizen follows up on clinical visits with personalized 1-on-1 support throughout pregnancy and postpartum.

Proactive

We follow up with patients across pregnancy and postpartum to catch issues before they escalate.

Personal

Every interaction is 1-on-1 with an experienced, trained registered nurse.

Customized in content

Each touchpoint is tailored to the patient's history and current stage.

Customized in timing

Touchpoints align to clinical milestones so check-ins happen when they change outcomes.

Closed-loop

Daily digest back to your team — patient status, flagged concerns, and what needs your attention.

How it works

One light setup. We handle the rest, every day.

A 30-minute configuration with your office manager wires the daily report from your EHR. From there, our team takes over the follow-up loop — and reports back to your team each evening.

Step 1

EHR delivers a daily patient report

Securely, via SFTP. No installs, no integration project.

Step 2

We plan the day's outreach

Each patient is routed to the right touchpoint based on stage, history, and risk.

Step 3

An RN reaches out

By phone or text, 1-on-1. Out-of-scope cases route back to your team.

Step 4

End-of-day digest

Patient status, flagged concerns, and what needs your attention — delivered to your team.
