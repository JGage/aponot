Home care that works for your workforce.

Employees in their 40s and 50s are carrying an invisible second job. Aponot is a benefit that makes it lighter — and keeps them at yours.

The problem inside your workforce

47% of adults in their 40s and 50s have a parent age 65 or older and are actively caring for them.

For most, that care is invisible to their employer — until it isn't. Missed meetings. Distracted workdays. A resignation letter.

The cost of caregiver-related absenteeism and turnover in US workplaces is measured in the tens of billions annually.

"By the time I realized my mom needed help, I was already burned out from trying to figure out where to start."
— Caregiver, 52
What it's costing you

Elder care benefits are becoming a competitive advantage

More employers are recognizing that eldercare support is a real lever for attracting, retaining, and getting the most out of their workforce. In 2016, roughly 15% of US employers offered a structured eldercare benefit. Today that number is approaching 25% — and climbing quickly as sandwich-generation demographics reshape workforces.

But most current offerings fall short of what employees actually need.

Navigation
Care coordinators on the phone. They help employees think through options and locate resources — but the employee still has to arrange the actual care themselves.
Companion visits
Social visits to the parent. Friendly, valuable for isolation — but not care. No help with bathing, mobility, medication, or the moments that actually cause a family crisis.
Referral lists
A vetted list of local agencies. The employee still calls around, screens providers, negotiates rates, and hopes someone picks up.
None of these deliver the thing employees actually need in a caregiving moment: real hands-on care, in the home, at a moment's notice. That's the gap Aponot fills.
How Aponot works

Employees log in, describe what their loved one needs, and confirm care — often in minutes. No phone tag. No hunting for an agency. No stress about vetting strangers.

Care is delivered through Aponot's vetted local agency network. All caregivers are professionally credentialed and background-checked.

Employers pay a predictable subscription. Employees can opt into additional coverage tiers via voluntary payroll deduction.
