The market

US home care is a massive and fast-growing industry, projected to exceed $140 billion by 2030. But growth alone doesn't help the people building it.

What's broken for founders

Setting up an efficient, legally-compliant home care agency or registry takes 6 to 10 months and tens of thousands of dollars.

Finding, training, managing, and retaining caregivers is a constant grind that eats time, energy, and money.

And finding leads, turning them into clients, and keeping those clients happy is no easier.

The existing options

Doing all of this alone is daunting.

Some founders try — and end up moving much more slowly and making much less money than they expected.

Others are drawn to franchises for the promise of a proven system — only to discover the promises are largely empty, and they're paying over 50% of their profit for very little.

Our mission

We're a group of ex-management consultants and MIT alumni who started home care agencies ourselves and learned the hard way.

We created Denizen to make the market work better for everyone in it — founders, clients, and caregivers alike.

Our model gives founders the operational support that actually matters, while preserving the freedom, flexibility, and long-term potential they wanted when they decided to build something of their own.
