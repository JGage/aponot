A referral that finally pays for the value you create.

For decades, non-skilled home care has sold one thing: hours. Referrals are lumpy, margins are thin, and the value you create — the fall that didn’t happen, the readmission you prevented, the ER trip avoided — shows up on someone else’s books.

Value-based care changes the unit of value from hours delivered to outcomes produced, and pays for them. Aponot brings that model to your agency — without asking you to build payer contracts, stand up a measurement system, or carry the risk alone. You keep doing what you do well. We make it worth more.

What joining the network gives you.

Steady dealflow

We aggregate demand from many payers and route matched, home-care-appropriate members to you. For a typical partner that’s a 50%+ increase in qualified pipeline — and it’s steady, not the boom-and-bust of chasing referrals.

Higher profitability

On value-based cases you earn for the care you deliver and share in the savings you create. That shared-savings layer on top of care revenue drives up to a 233% improvement in gross margin versus standard hourly work.

Pooled risk

No single agency can absorb the swing of outcomes on a handful of clients. We spread risk across many members and many agencies, so the variance is the network’s to carry, not yours. You deliver the care; we hold the risk.

Shared best practices

Every partner plugs into a common, evidence-based playbook — the post-discharge, medication, and escalation protocols a decade of value-based care has proven. We feed performance data back to your team so your caregivers measurably improve.

Better caregivers, across your whole book.

Here’s the part that compounds. The performance lift doesn’t stay on value-based cases. The training, protocols, and feedback your caregivers absorb travel with them to every client they serve — private-pay and Medicaid included.

One playbook raises quality across your entire roster, and it shows up exactly where it matters for an agency: stronger outcomes, higher client satisfaction, better caregiver retention, and more word-of-mouth referrals. You’re not standing up a separate value-based team off to the side — you’re upgrading the agency you already run.
